Your money, kept in your own Google Sheet.

Marlow is an iPhone app for tracking spending, budgets, savings goals, debts and investments, for yourself or your household.

Everything you enter is saved to a Google Sheet called Marlow in your own Google Drive. You can open it, edit it and download it at any time.

Marlow is in testing and not yet on the App Store.

What it does

  • Logs transactions from your money alertsAn iPhone Shortcut passes the bank, card and broker notifications you choose to the app, which turns each one into a row in your sheet.
  • Budgets, goals and debtsPlan monthly spending, track savings goals and pay down debts, with reminders before recurring payments are due.
  • InvestmentsRecord buys, sells and dividends; prices come from Google Finance inside your own sheet.
  • Shared with your householdInvite family members to the same sheet, each with their own accounts and goals.
  • A financial-independence coachAnswer a few questions and get a plan toward financial independence for your household.

Why it signs in with Google

When you sign in, Marlow creates one Google Sheet in your Drive and keeps your records there. It asks only for Google's drive.file permission, which lets it open the files it creates or you choose. It can't see the rest of your Drive, your email or your contacts.

Your financial records aren't stored on our servers. The app also uses your Google name and email address to show who you're signed in as and to share the sheet with family members you invite.

You can remove the app's access at any time in your Google Account, under Security › Third-party apps with account access. The Privacy Policy covers everything the app handles, including what its AI features send and that they send nothing until you allow them.
